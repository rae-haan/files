Panel 1 — New External Connection Pairs
Counter:
index=* sourcetype="bro:conn:json"
| where match(id_orig_h, "^172\.16\.")
| where NOT match(id_resp_h, "^172\.16\.")
| where NOT match(id_resp_h, "^127\.")
| where NOT match(id_resp_h, "^ff")
| stats min(_time) as first_seen by id_orig_h id_resp_h id_resp_p
| where first_seen > relative_time(now(), "-1h")
| stats count as new_external_pairs
Click-through table:
index=* sourcetype="bro:conn:json"
| where match(id_orig_h, "^172\.16\.")
| where NOT match(id_resp_h, "^172\.16\.")
| where NOT match(id_resp_h, "^127\.")
| where NOT match(id_resp_h, "^ff")
| stats min(_time) as first_seen count as sessions by id_orig_h id_resp_h id_resp_p service
| where first_seen > relative_time(now(), "-1h")
| eval first_seen=strftime(first_seen, "%Y-%m-%d %H:%M:%S")
| sort -first_seen
| table first_seen id_orig_h id_resp_h id_resp_p service sessions

Panel 3 — DDoS
Counter:
index=* sourcetype="bro:conn:json" earliest=-1m
| where NOT match(id_orig_h, "^172\.16\.")
| where match(id_resp_h, "^172\.16\.")
| stats count as conn_count dc(id_orig_h) as unique_sources by id_resp_h id_resp_p
| where conn_count > 100 OR unique_sources > 20
| stats count as ddos_targets
Click-through table:
index=* sourcetype="bro:conn:json" earliest=-1m
| where NOT match(id_orig_h, "^172\.16\.")
| where match(id_resp_h, "^172\.16\.")
| stats count as conn_count dc(id_orig_h) as unique_sources sum(orig_bytes) as total_bytes by id_resp_h id_resp_p
| where conn_count > 100 OR unique_sources > 20
| sort -conn_count
| table id_resp_h id_resp_p conn_count unique_sources total_bytes

Panel 4 — Exfiltration
Counter:
index=* sourcetype="bro:conn:json" earliest=-10m
| where match(id_orig_h, "^172\.16\.")
| where NOT match(id_resp_h, "^172\.16\.")
| stats sum(orig_bytes) as bytes_out by id_orig_h id_resp_h
| where bytes_out > 5000000
| stats count as exfil_alerts
Click-through table:
index=* sourcetype="bro:conn:json" earliest=-10m
| where match(id_orig_h, "^172\.16\.")
| where NOT match(id_resp_h, "^172\.16\.")
| stats sum(orig_bytes) as bytes_out sum(resp_bytes) as bytes_in count as sessions by id_orig_h id_resp_h
| where bytes_out > 5000000
| eval mb_out=round(bytes_out/1048576,2)
| sort -mb_out
| table id_orig_h id_resp_h mb_out bytes_in sessions

How The Self-Comparison Works
The first_seen > relative_time(now(), "-1h") line is doing all the work. Every time you run the search:

Splunk scans ALL data to find when each (src, dst, port) tuple first appeared ever
Then filters to only tuples whose FIRST APPEARANCE was within the last hour
Run it at 9am → shows tuples first seen after 8am
Run it at 3pm → shows tuples first seen after 2pm
No lookup table, no hardcoded times, no manual reset needed

Adjust the window to match how often you check:

Checking every hour → keep -1h
Checking every few hours → change to -3h
Coming back next day → change to -24h
